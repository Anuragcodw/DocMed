Legal & Privacy Governance

Privacy Policy

Transparent information on how DocMed collects, uses, and safeguards data for Patients and Healthcare Providers across our doctor appointment management platform.

Last Updated: August 18, 2026

1. Introduction

DocMed is an online doctor appointment management platform designed to connect patients with qualified healthcare professionals, streamline consultation scheduling, facilitate secure digital payments, and provide tele-consultation utilities. This Privacy Policy explains our data practices when you access or use DocMed via our web applications, APIs, or related services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide, maintain, and improve our appointment scheduling services. The categories of information collected include:

  • Patient Information: Full name, email address, phone number, date of birth, emergency contact details, and general health messages submitted during booking.
  • Doctor Information: Full name, medical degree, specialization, registration numbers (e.g. State Medical Council / National Medical Commission numbers), hospital affiliations, consultation fees, working days, and operational time slots.
  • Account Information: User credentials (username, hashed passwords), user role classifications (`patient`, `doctor`, or `staff`), profile pictures, and authentication logs.
  • Appointment Information: Selected date, time slot, appointment status (`pending`, `approved`, `rescheduled`, `cancelled`, `completed`), doctor notes, and consultation history.
  • Payment Information: Transaction amounts, payment status, invoice numbers, payment gateway identifiers (Razorpay order ID, payment ID, signature), and receipt metadata. (DocMed does not directly store full credit card numbers or banking PINs).
  • Medical & Prescription Information: Prescriptions generated by doctors, uploaded lab reports, AI analysis records, and health risk assessments requested by the user.
  • Location Information: City, state, latitude/longitude coordinates (when location features or nearby doctor searches are utilized).
  • Google Calendar Integration Data: OAuth 2.0 authorization tokens, Google Calendar account email, and event identifiers (`google_event_id`) used strictly to create, update, or cancel appointment events on the doctor's primary calendar.
  • Google Meet Integration Data: Generated Google Meet video conference URLs (`google_meet_link`) created via the Google Calendar API for online video consultations.
  • Email Notifications: Email dispatch logs, event triggers (booking confirmations, status changes, payment receipts), and delivery timestamps.
  • SMS & Push Notifications: Telephone numbers and audit logs stored in `NotificationLog` for SMS (via Twilio) and push notification delivery (via Firebase Cloud Messaging).

3. How We Use Information

We process personal and appointment data for specific, legitimate operational purposes:

  • Appointment Management: To verify slot availability, prevent double booking, issue appointment confirmations, and allow doctors to update consultation statuses.
  • Payment Processing: To generate invoices, verify transaction signatures via payment gateways (e.g. Razorpay), update payment statuses, and process receipts.
  • Notifications & Reminders: To deliver 24-hour and 2-hour automated reminders, status updates, and Google Meet video links via Email, SMS, and In-App notifications.

4. Data Storage and Security

Data is stored in secure database infrastructure supporting DocMed. We employ standard database permissions, hashed password storage (Django PBKDF2), role-based view decorators (`user_is_patient`, `user_is_doctor`), and environment variable segregation for external secret keys.

5. Third-Party Services

DocMed integrates with essential third-party service providers:

  • Google APIs (Google Calendar API & Google Meet): Used to synchronize confirmed appointments to doctor calendars and generate Google Meet video conference links. Google user data obtained via OAuth is used strictly for calendar event and Meet link management and is not shared or sold.
  • Razorpay: Used for processing payment transactions and verifying payment signatures in test/live mode.
  • Firebase (Google Firebase): Used for push notifications and optional authentication utilities.
  • Twilio: Used for sending transactional SMS notifications and appointment reminders.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal health or appointment information to third parties. Information is shared strictly between the patient and the assigned doctor for consultation purposes, or with necessary third-party infrastructure providers (such as Google APIs or Razorpay) to complete your requested transaction or meeting.

7. User Rights

Users have the right to access, review, and update their profile information through their account dashboard. Doctors may disconnect their Google Calendar integration at any time from their Doctor Settings.

8. Data Retention

We retain appointment records, invoices, and notification logs for as long as necessary to maintain accurate medical scheduling history, satisfy tax and legal recordkeeping requirements, and resolve operational disputes.

9. Cookies

DocMed uses essential session cookies (such as `sessionid` and `csrftoken`) to maintain authenticated sessions, secure HTTP POST forms against CSRF attacks, and remember user preferences.

10. Security Measures

We implement standard web application security controls, including Django's built-in session protection, CSRF token validation, password hashing, and role-based view permissions to protect patient and doctor records.

11. Children's Privacy

DocMed is intended for use by adults or guardians managing healthcare appointments. Minors under the age of 18 should use the platform only with the involvement and consent of a parent or legal guardian.

12. Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect platform enhancements or legal changes. The "Last Updated" date at the top of this page indicates when revisions take effect.
